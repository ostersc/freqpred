## Findings

**F1 — The binding constraint is the cost base, not the edge.**
Production LLM spend was **$11.67** this window against **+$0.27** of live
trading P&L — the system spent roughly **43× its gross weekly profit** on
inference. Cumulatively across four live weeks, trading is ≈ **−$3.96** while
LLM spend is ≈ **$45**. At ~1 contract per position no achievable improvement to
exits or gates closes that gap: the entire realised exit benefit for the whole
90-day live book (F3) is $13.74, which is a bit over one week of inference cost.
Last week's report said the constraint is stake, not edge quality. That is right
but incomplete — at the current per-contract edge (`signal-v11`, **−0.029**),
raising stake scales a negative number. **Both** the cost base and the sign of
the edge have to move before size helps.

### R1 — Let `assessment-v8` accrue against the rollout guard; do not retire the assessor yet

**Downgraded from "nominate retirement" after F6 was corrected.** The retirement
case was built entirely on `assessment-v6`, which was retired on 2026-07-25. The
live assessor has one production observation. Retiring a version we have not
measured, on evidence from the version it replaced, is not a defensible call.

- **Evidence**: F6/F6b. The flat-tax case is real but historical — v6:
  151/152 `size_down`, mean multiplier 0.776, realised sizing delta **−$1.43
  over 42 positions**, tilt +0.0187 [+0.002, +0.035] barely clearing zero. v8's
  entire history is **n=1** at multiplier 0.92. v4's realised delta was *positive*
  (+$0.39 over 51), so the sign is not even stable across versions. The skill's
  standing note (2026-07-25) — no assessor package has beaten a free
  direction×edge-band lookup on incremental AUC — still argues retirement is a
  live option, but it is not settled by this week's data.
- **Expected effect**: **$0 this week by construction.** The prize if v8 turns
  out to behave like v6 is ~**+$3.3/week** of avoided inference (v8 costs
  $0.0413/call; the last full week ran 76 assessor calls) plus ~+$0.7/week of
  avoided negative sizing delta. That prize is **contingent and currently
  unmeasured**, and note it may shrink a lot: post-`0805fc7` signal volume fell
  ~75%, so future assessor call volume — and therefore the saving — is likely
  well below 76 calls/week.
- **Risk**: waiting costs real money at ~$0.04/call if v8 is as flat as v6, and
  the guard needs enough positions to conclude anything, which at current entry
  volume could take weeks. The opposite risk is worse: retiring on v6 evidence
  would discard a version that may actually discriminate, and re-adopting later
  costs another full validation cycle.
- **Confidence**: **high** that the decision should wait; **low** on which way it
  resolves. Raised by v8 accruing ~40+ assessments with attached position
  outcomes.
- **Verification**: re-read section 6 next week for an `assessment-v8` row with
  a real `n`, tilt CI, and realised sizing delta, plus:
  ```bash
  docker exec freqpred-db-1 psql -U freqpred -d freqpred -c "SELECT q.prompt_version, count(*) AS n, round(avg(sa.size_multiplier)::numeric,3) AS mean_mult, round(sum(q.cost_usd)::numeric,2) AS cost FROM signal_assessments sa JOIN llm_queries q ON q.id=sa.llm_query_id WHERE q.prompt_version='assessment-v8' GROUP BY 1;"
  ```
- **Revert trigger** (i.e. escalate back to a retirement nomination): v8 reaches
  n≥40 with a tilt CI including zero **and** a mean multiplier below ~0.85 — the
  v6 signature of a flat tax. At that point it goes to the
  `freqpred/metrics/CLAUDE.md` gate as **multiplier fixed at 1.0, assessor call
  removed**, and the exposure caps must be re-checked first, since unit sizing
  raises per-trade exposure by 1/mean_mult.

### R2 — Nominate a benchmark of `signal-v11` against the `signal-v9` lineage

Not adoptable here: signal-prompt change, gated by `freqpred/signal/CLAUDE.md`,
which requires the full benchmark on the frozen eval set with a paired delta CI.

- **Evidence**: F4. `signal-v9` +0.056 [+0.045, +0.068] over 247 markets vs
  `signal-v11` −0.029 [−0.050, −0.008] over 79 markets; both CIs exclude zero,
  opposite signs, gap ≈ 0.085/contract. Stable across two consecutive reviews,
  with v11's market count growing 57 → 79 while staying negative.
- **Expected effect**: at 20 entries/week and 1 contract each, closing the full
  gap is worth ≈ **0.085 × 20 = +$1.70/week** (range +$1.06 to +$2.34 from the
  non-overlapping CI bounds ±). This is the only lever here that scales with
  stake rather than being capped — which matters precisely because of F1.
- **Risk**: the cohorts are **not exchangeable** — v9 is frozen and ran in a
  different period over a different market population, so the entire gap could
  be regime rather than prompt. Reverting to a v9-derived prompt could import
  whatever v10/v11 were built to fix; the v10 cohort at −0.200 shows this
  lineage can move sharply the wrong way. A benchmark run costs API dollars and
  may come back null.
- **Confidence**: **medium** that v11 is underperforming its predecessor;
  **low** that reverting the prompt is the right remedy. Raised by the frozen-set
  paired delta, which controls the market population the cohort comparison
  cannot.
- **Verification**: `scripts/benchmark_signals.py` with the frozen scenario bank
  and `--training-cutoff`; the gate is the paired Brier/edge delta CI, not the
  cohort table in F4.
- **Revert trigger**: n/a — an investigation, not a change. Drop it if the
  paired delta spans zero on the frozen set, and record that the F4 gap was
  regime.
