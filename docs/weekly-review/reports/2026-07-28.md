## Findings

**F1 — The binding constraint is the cost base, not the edge.**
Production LLM spend was **$11.67** this window against **+$0.27** of live
trading P&L — the system spent roughly **43× its gross weekly profit** on
inference. Cumulatively across four live weeks, trading is ≈ **−$3.96** while
LLM spend is ≈ **$45**. At ~1 contract per position no achievable improvement to
exits or gates closes that gap: the entire realised exit benefit for the whole
90-day live book (F3) is $13.74, which is a bit over one week of inference cost.
Last week's report said the constraint is stake, not edge quality. That is right
but incomplete — at the current per-contract edge (`signal-v11`, **−0.029**),
raising stake scales a negative number. **Both** the cost base and the sign of
the edge have to move before size helps.

**F2 — The direction effect has decayed; see "Last week's calls."** NO
+0.017 [−0.010, +0.041] over 52 markets, CI includes zero. Retired as an
actionable finding.

**F3 — Exits still beat holding, and the stoploss should stay off — but the
margin is thinner than last week.**
`force_exit:algo_exit` saved **+$13.74 [+0.009, +1.707]** over 15 live exits,
14 helped / 1 hurt. Note the lower CI bound is **+0.009** — this clears zero by
a hair and is one bad exit away from not clearing it. Signal exits saved
+$0.56 over 24, CI spanning zero.

### R1 — Nominate retiring the sizing assessor, starting by resolving why v8 writes no rows

Not adoptable here: this is an assessor change and goes through the gate in
`freqpred/metrics/CLAUDE.md`, which requires a validation run proposed to you.
The proposed arm is **multiplier fixed at 1.0, assessor call removed**.

- **Evidence**: F6. `assessment-v6` issued `size_down` on 151 of 152
  assessments at mean multiplier 0.776 with a realised sizing delta of
  **−$1.43 over 42 positions**; tilt +0.0187 [+0.002, +0.035] barely clears zero
  and describes a uniform haircut, not discrimination. Direct cost from the
  audit log: **76 calls / $3.37 in the prior full week** (~$0.044/call). The
  skill's standing note (2026-07-25) is that no assessor package has beaten a
  free direction×edge-band lookup on incremental AUC.
- **Expected effect**: **+$3.37/week** of avoided inference cost, plus a
  realised sizing delta that has been negative (−$1.43/42 positions ≈
  −$0.034/position, or ~−$0.68/week at 20 positions/week). Total ≈
  **+$3.4 to +$4.1/week**. Against a week that grossed +$0.27, the cost saving
  alone is **more than 12× the trading profit**. The cost side is an invoice,
  not an estimate — it does not depend on the tilt being real.
- **Risk**: if the tilt is genuinely positive, removing it costs the ~+$0.02/unit
  it contributes; v4's realised delta *was* +$0.39, so the sign is not settled
  across versions. Worst realistic case is losing a small real risk-control on
  the tail — sizing every position at full multiplier increases exposure per
  trade by ~29% (1/0.776), which interacts with the exposure caps and must be
  re-checked before it ships.
- **Confidence**: **high** on the cost saving, **medium** on the sizing delta.
  Raised by the gate's own run: a paired comparison of assessed vs unit sizing
  on the frozen set.
- **Verification**: `freqpred metrics assessor-audit` on the frozen set per
  `freqpred/metrics/CLAUDE.md`, plus this query for the realised cost:
  ```bash
  docker exec freqpred-db-1 psql -U freqpred -d freqpred -c "SELECT date_trunc('week', timestamp)::date AS wk, count(*) AS calls, round(sum(cost_usd)::numeric,2) AS cost FROM llm_queries WHERE query_type='signal_assessment' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1 DESC LIMIT 6;"
  ```
- **Revert trigger**: reinstate if, over 60+ positions sized at 1.0, live P&L per
  position is worse than the assessed cohort's with a CI excluding zero — or if
  a single position's loss exceeds the per-position cap that the 0.776 haircut
  had been holding down.
- **First step regardless of the gate**: resolve F6. If v8 is genuinely stalled,
  production has been sizing without an LLM assessor since 2026-07-25 and *the
  retirement experiment is already running by accident* — which would be the
  cheapest possible evidence, and needs to be recognised as data rather than
  left as a bug.

### R2 — Nominate a benchmark of `signal-v11` against the `signal-v9` lineage

Not adoptable here: signal-prompt change, gated by `freqpred/signal/CLAUDE.md`,
which requires the full benchmark on the frozen eval set with a paired delta CI.

- **Evidence**: F4. `signal-v9` +0.056 [+0.045, +0.068] over 247 markets vs
  `signal-v11` −0.029 [−0.050, −0.008] over 79 markets; both CIs exclude zero,
  opposite signs, gap ≈ 0.085/contract. Stable across two consecutive reviews,
  with v11's market count growing 57 → 79 while staying negative.
- **Expected effect**: at 20 entries/week and 1 contract each, closing the full
  gap is worth ≈ **0.085 × 20 = +$1.70/week** (range +$1.06 to +$2.34 from the
  non-overlapping CI bounds ±). This is the only lever here that scales with
  stake rather than being capped — which matters precisely because of F1.
- **Risk**: the cohorts are **not exchangeable** — v9 is frozen and ran in a
  different period over a different market population, so the entire gap could
  be regime rather than prompt. Reverting to a v9-derived prompt could import
  whatever v10/v11 were built to fix; the v10 cohort at −0.200 shows this
  lineage can move sharply the wrong way. A benchmark run costs API dollars and
  may come back null.
- **Confidence**: **medium** that v11 is underperforming its predecessor;
  **low** that reverting the prompt is the right remedy. Raised by the frozen-set
  paired delta, which controls the market population the cohort comparison
  cannot.
- **Verification**: `scripts/benchmark_signals.py` with the frozen scenario bank
  and `--training-cutoff`; the gate is the paired Brier/edge delta CI, not the
  cohort table in F4.
- **Revert trigger**: n/a — an investigation, not a change. Drop it if the
  paired delta spans zero on the frozen set, and record that the F4 gap was
  regime.

- **Verification**: the `-- this window only --` block in next week's section 2,
