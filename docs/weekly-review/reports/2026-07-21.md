## Findings

**F1 — Direction is the dominant effect, and it is not close.** (`by direction`)
NO: 39 markets, 88.6% hit at avg ask 0.753 → **+0.133/contract [+0.110, +0.156]**.
YES: 31 markets, 33.6% hit at avg ask 0.523 → **−0.187/contract [−0.225, −0.147]**.
Both CIs exclude zero on the cumulative window. It is not one lucky cell: crossing
direction with edge band (first signal per market), all three NO bands with n≥6 are
positive (+0.157, +0.091, +0.048) and all three YES bands with n≥9 are negative
(−0.147, −0.271, −0.170). That is 62 of 70 markets pointing the same way. The two
dissenting cells are the `>40` bands at n=2 and n=6 — below any bar.

**Counter-evidence, stated plainly:** live YES P&L by week runs −1.02, −9.06,
**+1.54, +1.18**. The two most recent weeks went the *opposite* way to the
cumulative finding. Those are ~10-position samples at ~1 contract each and cannot
overturn 70 markets, but they are the only real-money evidence and they disagree.

**F2 — signal-v11 is worse than signal-v9 on realised profit.** (`by prompt version`)
v9: +0.056/contract [+0.045, +0.068] over 247 markets. v11: −0.032 [−0.058, −0.008]
over 57. A −0.088/contract swing, both CIs excluding zero. Heavily confounded — the
cohorts ran in different periods against different market conditions, and this
review has no way to separate prompt from regime. That is exactly what the frozen
scenario bank in `freqpred/signal/CLAUDE.md` exists for.

**F3 — The live exit policy is beating every alternative tested.** (sections 2, 3)
`force_exit:algo_exit` saved **+$13.23 [+0.114, +1.895]** over 13 live exits,
12 helped / 1 hurt. Signal exits saved +$2.27 over 19 (CI spans zero).

*Updated 2026-07-26 with candle data.* The two MAE-based sweep arms disagreed in
sign, so this was previously the weakest part of the finding. The candle-based
sweep — real price paths, **74/74 live positions covered**, no censoring and no
survivor conditioning — settles it: actual **−$4.23** beats every stoploss
threshold (−$7.46 to −$18.23 worse) *and* beats no-stoploss by **$16.57**. The
current policy is the best arm tested.

This still contradicts the pooled view, and the candle data makes the gap wider,
not narrower: pooled (232/430 covered, overwhelmingly paper) says removing the
stoploss entirely would have been **+$95.01**. **All 185 of those stoplosses are
paper.** Live has had three ever, in March. Acting on the pooled number would mean
re-tuning a rule that has not fired in live in four months.

**F4 — The 19.5% neutral-fallback rate is by design, not an outage.** 248 of 263
`price_moved` neutral assessments have no LLM call behind them, versus 3 of 73
`scheduled` ones. That is the documented carry-forward path in
`assess_signal_context()` for clones whose evidence set is unchanged. Recorded so a
future review does not re-raise it as a failure.

## Recommendations

### R1 — Restrict entries to NO on KXTRUMPSAY

Add a `directions: list[str]` field to `StrategyConfig` (default `["YES", "NO"]`,
so no existing strategy changes) and set it to `["NO"]` for `PoliticsEdgeStrategy`.
There is currently no config lever for this, so it is ~10 lines plus a test.

- **Evidence**: F1. 70 markets, both direction CIs exclude zero, consistent across
  every edge band with n≥6. Live corroboration since trading began: 64 YES entries
  netted −$7.36, 33 NO entries netted +$3.46.
- **Expected effect**: 14 of last week's 22 closed positions were YES, at ~1
  contract each. Removing a −0.187/contract expectation on ~14 contracts/week is
  **+$2.60/week (range +$2.06 to +$3.15** from the CI). Against a total weekly
  result of +$4.43, that is large in relative terms and trivial in absolute ones.
- **Risk**: cuts trade volume roughly 60%, which slows every other measurement
  including this one. The last two live weeks had YES profitable, so if the effect
  is regime rather than structural we forgo those trades. Concentrating entirely in
  NO also concentrates exposure in one payoff shape — NO's profit comes from
  high-priced favourites (avg ask 0.84 hitting 94–100%), which is precisely the
  profile that suffers a large loss when a favourite fails.
- **Confidence**: **medium.** The signal-level evidence is strong and well-powered;
  the live evidence is thin and currently pointing the other way. What would raise
  it: two more weekly reviews with the YES CI still excluding zero.
- **Verification**: `by direction` in next week's section 5, plus
  `SELECT direction, count(*), sum(pnl) FROM positions WHERE mode='live' AND status='closed' AND exit_time >= <week start> GROUP BY 1;`
- **Revert trigger**: back it out if the cumulative YES edge CI includes zero in any
  later review, **or** if the counterfactual YES signals we declined would have made
  money in three consecutive weeks.

### R2 — Nominate signal-v11 → a benchmark run against v9

Not adoptable here. Route through `freqpred/signal/CLAUDE.md`: build a scenario
bank and run v9 as candidate against v11 as incumbent with `--training-cutoff` set.

- **Evidence**: F2. −0.088/contract swing, 247 vs 57 markets, both CIs exclude zero.
- **Expected effect**: if the gap is prompt rather than regime, recovering it is
  worth ~+0.088/contract on every trade — roughly **+$2/week at current volume**,
  and the only finding here that scales with size rather than being capped by it.
- **Risk**: the estimate is confounded and could be entirely regime. A benchmark run
  costs real API dollars against the daily cap and may return a null result.
- **Confidence**: **low** on the cause, **high** that the realised gap is real.
- **Verification**: `scripts/benchmark_signals.py --candidate-model ... --training-cutoff ...`;
  the paired Brier delta with bootstrap CI is the gate, not this table.
- **Revert trigger**: n/a — this is an investigation, not a change. Drop it if the
  benchmark's CI on the paired delta spans zero.

### R3 — Change nothing about exits or the stoploss

An explicit do-nothing, because the pooled data argues loudly for the opposite.

- **Evidence**: F3. Live algo_exit +$13.23 [+0.114, +1.895] over 13 exits; zero live
  stoplosses in 90 days; and the candle sweep at 74/74 live coverage puts the
  actual policy ahead of every threshold and of no-stoploss by $16.57.
- **Expected effect**: $0 by construction. The value is the ~$158 of paper-driven
  "improvement" — or the ~$95 the pooled candle sweep credits to *removing* the
  stoploss — not chased into a rule that does not fire in live.
- **Risk**: if live volume grows and stoplosses start firing, this conclusion has no
  live evidence behind it — it rests on 13 algo exits.
- **Confidence**: **high** for current conditions (raised from medium: the candle
  sweep removed the MAE arms' sign disagreement), **low** as volume scales.
- **Verification**: section 2's `stoploss` row appearing at all in a live-scoped
  review is the signal that this needs revisiting.
- **Revert trigger**: five or more live stoploss exits in a window, or a live
  stoploss row with a negative `saved` whose CI excludes zero.
